A little about me

I'm an Associate AI/ML Engineer and a Computer Science graduate of FAST-NUCES (Dean's List). I work at the point where models meet real clients: vision systems that inspect damaged vehicles, Claude-powered tools that read engineering documents, multi-agent RAG for finance teams, and the Airflow pipelines that feed them.

What I care about most is honest evaluation. A good demo isn't enough for me. I dig into where a model fails, whether that's shortcut learning, leaky metrics or the wrong checkpoint, fix it, and explain the result in plain language.

Away from work I build things for fun: Urdu NLP tools, a gesture-controlled Spotify player, and a Ghibli-inspired bookshelf. I've also led events for my university's biggest festival. 📸
